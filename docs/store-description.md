# Elemental Command — Steam Store Description

## Short Description (30 words)

Command five elemental forces in strategic hex-grid battles. Build your party, master Fire, Water, Grass, Light, and Dark synergies, and conquer enemy formations — one command at a time.

---

## Full Description

**Elemental Command** is a single-player tactical strategy game that challenges you to think two steps ahead with every command you give. Choose a party of four from a roster of elemental units — each aligned with Fire, Water, Grass, Light, or Dark — and deploy them on a hex-grid battlefield where positioning, elemental affinity, and timing are the difference between victory and ruin. Simple to pick up, demanding to master, the game rewards players who learn to read an enemy formation before they act.

At the heart of Elemental Command is its elemental synergy system. Units of matching or complementary elements generate bonus multipliers when placed near allies, turning careful party composition into a puzzle worth solving before the first move is made. As you clear stages, new characters unlock that shift the entire meta of your roster, encouraging fresh approaches and forcing you to reconsider combinations you thought were settled. A dynamic gem-driven action economy ensures no two turns feel the same, even across dozens of hours of play.

The stage structure respects your time while never letting you coast. A focused boot sequence drops you directly into the stage-select map, and each battle is designed to reach a decisive moment within fifteen minutes. Progressing deeper into the campaign introduces enemy formations that punish passive play and reward aggressive elemental reads — a design philosophy inspired by tile-matching strategy and modern tactics games. Whether you are chasing a clean clear on the highest difficulty or experimenting with off-meta party builds, Elemental Command delivers strategic depth in every session.

---

## Key Features

- **Five-Element System** — Fire, Water, Grass, Light, and Dark units each carry unique stats and elemental bonuses. Matching placements yield 20%+ damage multipliers.
- **Hex-Grid Tactical Combat** — Position your party of four across a hex board; formation and adjacency matter as much as individual unit strength.
- **Elemental Synergy Combos** — Discover powerful element pairings that chain bonuses, shift battle momentum, and unlock hidden potential in your roster.
- **Growing Roster & Stage Progression** — Unlock new elemental units as you advance through campaign stages, each changing the strategic calculus.
- **Focused Session Design** — Every stage is built for 10–15 minute battles. Drop in, command, and step away — or stay and push for a perfect run.
- **Strategic Depth Without Clutter** — Clean UI and tight turn structure keep the focus on decisions, not menus.
