# Elemental Command — Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** 2026-09-05
**Developer:** Stoicent
**Game:** Elemental Command (v0.7.0)
**Contact:** koheag2@stoicent.com

---

## Overview

Elemental Command is a singleplayer, offline game. It does not collect, transmit, store, or share any personal data. This policy explains what the game does and does not do with respect to user information.

---

## Data We Do Not Collect

Elemental Command does **not** collect any of the following:

- Personal identifiers (name, email address, account credentials)
- Device identifiers (device ID, advertising ID, IP address)
- Location data (precise or approximate)
- Usage analytics or telemetry
- Gameplay statistics or session data
- Crash reports transmitted to external servers
- Any data that could be used to identify or track you

---

## No Network Activity

Elemental Command operates entirely offline. The game:

- Does not connect to the internet during normal gameplay
- Does not connect to any external server, analytics service, or third-party API
- Does not transmit data of any kind to the developer or any third party

---

## Local Save Data

Elemental Command stores game progress locally on your device (save files, settings). This data:

- Never leaves your device
- Is not accessible to the developer
- Can be deleted at any time by removing the game's save directory

---

## No In-App Purchases

Elemental Command does not offer in-app purchases, premium currency, loot boxes, or any monetization system that involves user financial data.

---

## No Third-Party SDKs or Advertising

Elemental Command does not include:

- Advertising networks or ad SDKs
- Analytics SDKs (e.g., Firebase, Google Analytics, Unity Analytics)
- Social media SDKs
- Any third-party library that accesses or transmits user data

---

## Children's Privacy

Because Elemental Command collects no data of any kind, it is safe for players of all ages and complies with COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act) and applicable regional children's privacy regulations.

---

## Steam Platform

If you purchase Elemental Command through Steam, Valve Corporation's privacy policy applies to your Steam account and transaction. That policy is separate from this one and governs how Valve handles your data. Stoicent receives no personal data from Steam.

---

## Changes to This Policy

If Elemental Command is ever updated to collect data (for example, optional cloud saves), this policy will be updated and a new effective date will be published before that version ships.
